Privacy Policy — Sparky

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Sparky is a personal AI assistant application operated by Terry Mlangeni for his own private use. It connects to the WhatsApp Business Platform so the assistant can exchange WhatsApp messages with its operator.

What data is processed

  • WhatsApp message content exchanged between the operator and the assistant's number.
  • Message delivery metadata (message IDs, timestamps, delivery statuses).
  • Basic account identifiers needed to operate the integration (phone numbers, WhatsApp account IDs).

How the data is used

Data is used only to operate the assistant for its operator: receiving messages, replying, and keeping a private log of the exchanges. Data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing, and it is not used to train models.

Storage and retention

Message logs are stored on the operator's private systems and kept only as long as they are useful to the operator. They can be deleted at any time on the operator's instruction.

Your choices

Anyone who has exchanged messages with the assistant's number may ask for those records to be deleted by contacting the operator at the email below.
